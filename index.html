How this map is made

PC1 rises with polar surface area, H-bond donors and acceptors and size. PC2 rises with size, aromatic rings and cLogP, and falls with Fsp3. The axes come from a principal component analysis of eight RDKit properties over 1,294 FDA-approved drugs, so any new structure can be placed on the same map.
